setup.py downloads this pinned archive, verifies its digest, and extracts
Scheme source and the license into .deps. No dependency download happens at
demo startup. --archive can use a previously downloaded copy.

The generated (sdl3) library selects upstream definitions used by these demos
and their dependencies, preserving their source order. Unrelated unfinished
bindings and duplicate procedure names are excluded. Its changes are an explicit
SDL3_LIBRARY path and the missing SDL_KeyboardEvent.scancode field. The path and
keyboard fixes follow the existing manse integration. SDL_SetMainReady is absent
from upstream and is bound directly in canvas.sls. No custom C shim is used.

The upstream binding targets SDL 3.4.12. Tested here with SDL 3.4.16 and
Chez 10.4.1 on macOS arm64. Treat other platforms as untested.
